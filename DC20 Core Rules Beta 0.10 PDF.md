# **Maneuvers**

Techniques chapter has been removed, and now there are only Maneuvers.

Maneuvers have been rewritten from the ground up in a new format to align with the current rules for DC20, including the new Resource Values & Progression.

We are categorizing Maneuvers similarly as before:

- **Attack:** Maneuvers that deal damage.
- **Defense:** Maneuvers that prevent or mitigate damage.
- **Grapple:** Maneuvers that control other creatures using physical force
- **Utility:** Maneuvers that improve mobility, improve combat potential, apply effects to other creatures, or control other creatures through force of will (such as Taunting or Intimidation).

# <span id="page-228-0"></span>**Spellcaster Chapter**

The Chapter has been renamed from the Spellcasting Chapter to the Spellcaster Chapter, and has been rewritten for organization and clarity.

- **51** Spell Tags have been added to the game.
- Spell Schools have been restructured and updated.

#### New Schools

- **Astromancy:** time, space, gravity.
- **Conjuration:** Creating physical material from nothing.
- **Divination:** Creating information from nothing.
- **Elemental:** manipulating the elements.
- **Enchantment:** Alteration of Mental (buff & debuff).
- **Invocation:** positive energy (life and spirit).
- **Nullification:** negative energy (destroy matter or spirit).
- **Transmutation:** Alteration of Physical (change form, shape, or matter).

#### Removed Schools

- **Chronomancy** is now part of Astromancy, and is also represented by the Time Spell Tag.
- **Destruction** is now spread across Elemental (elemental damage), Invocation (radiant damage spells), Nullification (umbral damage spells), and Enchantment (psychic damage spells). It's also represented by several Spell Tags associated with Damage Types (i.e. Fire, Cold, Piercing, etc.)
- **Restoration** is now part of Invocation, which covers life energy. It's also represented by the Cleansing, Healing, and Resurrection Spell Tags.
- **Illusion**, **Necromancy**, and **Protection** have become Spell Tags that are added to Spells with associated effects.

#### Components

- All Spells have Verbal and Somatic Components, unless stated otherwise. Specific Spells may have a Material Component.
- Verbal Components
- Creatures that can hear you know you're casting a Spell if you have a Verbal Component.

#### Advanced Spellcasting

- The rules for Spell Duels & Combo Casting have been rewritten to tie directly into each other to make it easier to adjudicate what happens when a Combo Casted spell is Spell Dueled.
- Includes Wild Magic Table.

#### Spell Focuses

• Spell Focus are a new item that Spellcasters can use to cast Spells. These are not required for casting Spells, but add a small measure of depth to your Spellcasting. They function similar to a Martial's Weapon, where they can make Attacks on their own (using Unarmed Strikes) but a Weapon comes with Weapon Properties (Reach, Versatile, Impact, etc.). Similarly, a Spell Focus has Focus Properties that add benefits to a Spellcaster's Spell (Channeling, Warded, Reach, etc.).

#### Attack Manuevers

#### Sonic Damage

Removed from the game.

#### **Issue**

Right now Sonic damage represents high frequency sound waves that cause pain in your ears (if you aren't deaf) and potentially damage unique creatures that are sensitive to high frequency vibrations (venom symbiote). This makes it a very niche form of damage.

#### **Solution**

We can eliminate Sonic damage by changing how we frame high frequency attacks:

- they force you to make a Save against being Deafened, Dazed, or something else.
- they deal Bludgeoning if its part of a wave of force that hits you (explosion)
  - this type of attack may deal double damage (or just True damage) against objects and creatures sensitive to high frequency vibrations (glass and venom symbiote).
- these things might instead have Vulnerable to damage from **High Frequency Sound**.
- Internal damage would be True damage (organs and stuff).

By framing things this way, Sonic damage no longer needs to exist, simplifying the number of damage types in the game.

#### Rituals

Rituals are no longer a Spell Tag. Instead, they are a group of activities (not Spells) that all PCs can learn and perform (Identify, Mending, etc.). The classic Alarm Spell is now a Ritual that can now be performed by a Martial who may just use their time performing the Ritual to set up a series of trip wires and bells to alert them of a nearby intruder.
